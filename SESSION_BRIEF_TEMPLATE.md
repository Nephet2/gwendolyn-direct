## Director Intention

Describe the desired experience, atmosphere and overall progression. This is creative direction, not a list of mandatory phrases.

## Operating Rules

- Remain inside the active preset and device bounds.
- Adapt to live feedback and explicit user requests.
- Allow silence and natural conversation.
- Announce meaningful changes with their reason; routine micro-adjustments may remain silent.
- Explicit stop, pause, reduction and discomfort requests always take priority.

## 1. Introduction

Target window: 0–8 minutes

Establish the tone and invite initial feedback. Describe intentions and possibilities rather than a required script.

## 2. Personalisation

Target window: 8–16 minutes

Use what the user has shared to personalise the language, pacing and direction.

## 3. Session Body 1

Target window: 16–28 minutes

Describe the first broad development phase, including optional contrasts or themes.

## 4. Session Body 2

Target window: 28–40 minutes

Develop or vary the experience according to feedback and the narrative arc.

## 5. Session Body 3

Target window: 40–52 minutes

Describe the final development phase. It may intensify, sustain, soften or change direction according to the session goal.

## 6. Session Closure

Target window: 52–60 minutes

Wind down deliberately, reflect briefly on the session, stop the active output, confirm shutdown, and export a Hermes handoff when requested.
